Addressing Surveillance

Your Digital Identity

Every time we use the internet, we leave behind information about ourselves. This can include the websites we visit, the things we search for, the videos we watch, and the content we interact with.

Individually, these actions might seem insignificant. However, when collected together, they can reveal patterns about our interests, habits, and behaviour.

Digital platforms can use this information to build profiles of their users. These profiles can help determine what content, advertisements, and recommendations appear on our screens.

But how much can someone really learn about you from a few simple interactions?
