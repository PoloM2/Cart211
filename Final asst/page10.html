Addressing Surveillance

Every Click Counts

Every time we use the internet, we leave behind small pieces of information. Clicking on a video, liking a post, visiting a website or searching for something can all create data.

Most of these actions seem unimportant on their own. However, websites and online platforms can collect them and look for patterns in our behaviour.

For example, if someone regularly watches videos about gaming, searches for new technology and visits gaming websites, platforms may begin to understand that these are some of their interests.

This information can then be used to recommend similar videos, suggest products or show advertisements that match what someone has been looking at.

Even when we are not directly sharing personal information, our everyday actions can reveal more about us than we might expect.

SMALL ACTIONS, BIG PICTURE

One click might not reveal much about a person. But when hundreds or thousands of clicks are collected over time, they can help create a detailed picture of someone's interests and habits.

Think about how many times you click, search, watch or scroll in a single day. How much information could those actions reveal when they are all put together?
